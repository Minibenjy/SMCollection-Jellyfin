Auto Thumbnails

Gives a thumbnail to anything that does not have one, taken from the content itself: page 0 of a comic, book or PDF, a frame from a video, or the first child's image for series, seasons and collections. Existing artwork is left alone unless you ask for a regenerate.

What to process

Regenerating also replaces artwork that came from Comic Vine, TheMovieDb or any other scraper with page 0 of the file. It cannot be undone without running the scraper again.
